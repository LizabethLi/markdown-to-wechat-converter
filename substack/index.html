Know the boundaries

  • Remote image hosts that block browser access may prevent the image binary from entering the clipboard.
  • Substack does not provide a native table block, so Markdown tables are copied as images.
  • A publication can choose custom title and body fonts, while the converter previews Substack's default article theme.
  • The tool formats content; it does not sign in, schedule, or publish on your behalf.

Frequently asked questions

Does Substack support raw Markdown paste?

Markdown structures may be interpreted inconsistently. This tool copies the rendered result as rich content instead of relying on Substack to parse the source.

Can formulas and Mermaid diagrams survive paste?

They are rendered first and converted into portable visual assets for the clipboard workflow.

Is my draft uploaded?

No. Conversion runs in the browser. Your article and local images are not sent to this project’s server.
