Frequently asked questions

Why do LinkedIn and Substack use the same converter option?

Both accept a similar portable rich-text clipboard payload, so they share one formatter. Their SEO pages remain separate because their publishing intent and audience are different.

Can I use this for a normal LinkedIn post?

Yes for drafting and plain formatting, but very long content is better suited to LinkedIn Articles and platform limits still apply.

Does the tool send content to LinkedIn?

No. It prepares content in your browser and places it on the clipboard. You control the final paste and publication.
